Buylist shipping research — checked October 4, 2026

Recommended service: quote USPS Ground Advantage in a sturdy card container inside a snug corrugated outer box; compare Priority Mail weight-based/Flat Rate and UPS Ground for larger shipments. USPS Ground Advantage includes tracking and $100 insurance; additional coverage costs extra. Use actual packed weight/dimensions, origin ZIP, dealer destination and ship date. No exact quote is possible from card quantity alone. Prefer commercial online label rates. Do not use an untracked letter for dealer batches.

Observed USPS webpage figures (indicative, recheck on label date): Ground Advantage retail starts8.40; Priority Small Flat Rate Box12.95commercial/14.65retail; Medium22.02commercial/25.80retail. A working small-batch allowance15 total, plus insurance above included coverage, is a planning assumption rather than a quote. Larger batch25 plus extra insurance until measured. Existing app10 CKbatch allowance is illustrative and excludes any unentered insurance/material costs.

CK: invoice-order, unsleeved singles, sturdy container, no movement; tracking/insurance encouraged. Postmark within7days. Current receiving address Monroe WA98272; confirm checkout address. SCG: approval first, postmark Sell List within2businessdays; unsleeved/no top-loaders, invoice-order; tracking/insurance; Roanoke VA24012. Multiple separately labeled requests may share a parcel. SCG Ship+Sell collection service has10% processing fee (<10000); do not apply this to standard itemized Sell List workflow.

Model: dealer batch net=sum eligible cash bids - postage - protective supplies - extra insurance - applicable payout charges. Fixed shipment expense once per dealer parcel, not per card. Marginal added-card postage often zero until weight/box/insurance tier changes. Splitting between dealers only when incremental cash gain exceeds additional parcel costs and worthwhile effort. Separate channel preference from timing/hold assessment. Show quoted/estimated/actual batch costs and keep source/date. In-person event handoff has zero parcel postage but requires buyer attendance/preapproval confirmation, not an assumed upcoming event vendor.
