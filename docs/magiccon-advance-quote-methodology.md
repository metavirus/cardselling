# MagicCon: price first, verification at handoff

Owner requirement recorded October 4, 2026. Target: Atlanta, November 13–15,
2026. This is a proposed selection and quote workflow, not an accepted sale,
reservation, guaranteed vendor service, or authorization to contact buyers.

The event list should be a negotiated purchase proposal with exact items and
an itemized or precisely scoped bundle cash price agreed before transport. A list that merely earns an appointment for a
buyer to browse and discover prices is not a successful outcome. A buyer may
verify identity, count and condition at handoff; substantive pricing should
already be agreed. The owner may still decline a sale.

## Build the proposal from canonical inventory

Use the post-reconciliation SQL stock, available quantities and accepted owner
identity corrections. Never reconstruct inventory from a retired spreadsheet.
Freeze a dated proposal version; later scans create an amendment, not silent
changes to the packet. Collapse identical market variants for quoting but retain
the contributing lot IDs and location for physical allocation.

Every market variant is exact Scryfall printing, set, collector number,
treatment, finish, language and condition. Record quantities offered separately
from quantity on hand, reserved and already allocated. A buyer's shared capacity
is consumed once across matching lots. Mostly NM is an owner assertion, not a
guarantee of each dealer's grading outcome.

## Select for worthwhile negotiation, not highest retail price

Classify each available variant with individualized AI judgment supported by
dated observations and deterministic dollar calculations:

1. **Competitive dealer staples:** real cash demand and capacity; repeated sales
   or strong functional demand suggest several buyers can use the stock. Seek
   competing cash bids without assuming popularity guarantees a premium.
2. **Specialist premium candidates:** exact-treatment sales support collector
   value, ordinary buylist is weak/missing, and a buyer has demonstrated relevant
   appetite. Special art or Japanese language alone is not evidence of scarcity.
3. **Efficient fillers:** easy buylist wins that can join an already worthwhile
   approved batch without separate sorting/negotiating work.
4. **Retain / patient self-sale:** supported timing thesis or meaningful credible
   self-sale upside; keep these out unless a quote beats the retained alternative.
5. **Research before proposal:** consequential comp mismatch, absent cash capacity,
   stale quote or unsupported high-price hypothesis. Do focused work only when it
   can change the selection; missing evidence is not an automatic exclusion from
   all future event quotes.

For each line show best current executable mail-buylist alternative (grade,
capacity and shipment allocation included), credible self-sale net range, patient
sale case when supported, and incremental event net. Gross market references
are context; they are not cash the owner can collect. eBay candidate aggregates
are not accepted exact-grade transaction facts without review.

Keep channel and timing separate: a strong dealer premium may favor selling to a
dealer whenever sold, while a genuine demand catalyst may still favor waiting.
AI must state the strongest argument against its proposed route and the fact
most likely to change it. Rank by credible total dollars and decision value,
not a blended score or percentage spread on a tiny card.

Do not invent an owner hourly rate or required profit threshold. Show the extra
dollars, preparation work, expected handoff burden and uncertainty together.
Use marginal selling costs because attendance is already contemplated; do not
charge the entire trip to cards, and do not treat time at booths as free.

## Price anchors and private acceptance conditions

For a variant and quantity, calculate:

`event_net = accepted_quantity * confirmed_cash_unit_price - incremental_event_selling_costs`

`event_advantage = event_net - best_credible_alternative_net`

Keep the minimum acceptable price private. It should follow the best alternative
the owner is actually willing to execute, including grade uncertainty, effort
and timing. A patient self-sale scenario is not automatically a guaranteed
minimum. An unsupported future target is not a floor. Without a chosen effort
tradeoff or evidence-backed price range, show a break-even range rather than
pretending an exact reservation price has been accepted.

Request buyer bids initially; use supported exact-sale bands for negotiation.
Do not send the owner's private floors or full AI research narrative to buyers.
Do not infer USD bids from EUR pages or apply a currency conversion to another
market's retail references. Ask for explicit USD cash purchase amounts.

## Qualify the process before sending a full collection

Prepared request text, for later owner-authorized outreach:

> I am preparing an itemized sale for MagicCon Atlanta. I want a USD cash bundle or itemized price
> and accepted quantities agreed in writing before bringing cards. At the event,
> review should be limited to confirming the listed printing, quantity and
> condition. Can you provide that process, quote validity through my delivery
> window, payment terms, and a realistic processing-time estimate? I am not
> seeking a collection appraisal or an appointment where prices are first set
> after browsing the cards. Please identify any exceptions before I prepare a
> packet.

Start with a few buyer-fit representative lines if requested, then a concise
tailored tranche. Compare a small number of qualified buyers; avoid distributing
hundreds of low-value lines to every booth. An estimate-only reply is not a
confirmed quote. If nobody agrees, use mail buylists/patient self-sale and keep
cards home rather than relying on sunk effort to justify the unwanted process.

## Required packet and response contract

Buyer packet fields: proposal ID/version/date; stable line ID; card name; set and
collector number; treatment; finish; language; asserted condition; offered
quantity; Scryfall/card image link; relevant close-up photos for material grade
questions; and buyer response columns for USD cash unit price, accepted quantity,
line total and exception notes. Include a compact cover summary and delivery
window. Physical lot locations and private floors remain internal.

Record every reply as a separate dated quote snapshot, preserving the original
message. Required terms: buyer identity/event presence, matched exact variant,
accepted quantity, currency, cash versus credit, fees, payout method/timing,
expiry/timezone, named delivery window, quote status, permitted grading adjustments
and refusal conditions. Bundle offers require an explicit allocation or an
all-or-nothing flag; a bundle total is not fabricated into independent card bids.

Statuses: draft; buyer asked; indicative estimate; confirmed conditional quote;
owner accepted/reserved; expired/superseded; verified at handoff; finalized/paid.
These are proposed workflow fields, not a claim that all exist in the current
app. A conditional written quote is not called legally binding without its actual
terms. Accept clearly scoped bundle totals without fabricating per-card allocations. Reject arbitrary repricing, undefined whole-box scope and unexplained
quantity reductions as incompatible with preparation assumptions.

## Decide, pack, verify, reconcile

Refresh evidence when building the first tranche and again near acceptance.
Confirm buyer capacity and expiry; a historical high is not a current offer.
Approve line allocations only once, preserving owner decisions separately from
AI recommendations. Split buyers only when the extra net justifies additional
sorting, queues and handoffs. Tie-break with simpler execution.

Before travel, produce buyer-sorted manifests, accepted terms and a short list
of exceptions. Label and count the agreed groups; leave unquoted speculative
stock at home. At handoff verify only the agreed fields. Any deduction must name
the affected line, grade, quantity and resulting dollars; seller can withdraw
that line. A general market repricing is a new offer, not silent confirmation.

Afterward record accepted copies, rejected/returned copies, final prices,
actual preparation and handoff time, costs, payout and remaining stock. Reserve
copies before delivery, finalize the sale only for accepted copies and reconcile
payment independently. Preserve rejected quote history for future buyer-fit
analysis; do not subtract offered copies from inventory merely because a packet
was sent.

## What completion looks like

The deliverable is a ranked, internally priced candidate tranche plus tailored
buyer packets and a comparison of actual written replies. The travel list is
only accepted allocations with confirmed terms. Today this document establishes
the methodology and policy gaps; it does not claim a final card shortlist,
buyer agreement, outreach, booking or sale. New inventory and fresh economics
must feed the candidate tranche before it is published.

## Mixed-bundle leverage: owner clarification

Owner October 4: desirable cards may improve a buyer’s willingness to purchase
low-desirability inventory. Do not liquidate a whole lot at a lowball total, and
do not exclude weaker cards automatically when good anchors make the bundle work.

Build a desirable core plus optional add-on modules (slow movers, weak individual
buylist coverage, inexpensive fillers). Request core-only and core-plus-module
bids from the same buyer on the same dated terms. The difference is an observed
marginal bundle bid, not an invented per-card valuation:

`addon_increment = mixed_bundle_cash - core_only_cash`

`addon_advantage = addon_increment - credible_addon_alternative_net - extra_preparation_cost`

Also compare the mixed total against credible alternatives for the entire set.
A seemingly generous add-on allocation does not help if the desirable core is
underpriced. Track core value retained and whole-bundle cash independently. Do
not accept a buyer’s arbitrary allocation as proof that weak cards gained value;
matched core-only and mixed quotes are the stronger test. If only a mixed quote
is available, evaluate the total without claiming proven cross-subsidy.

Use modules sufficiently worthwhile to handle as a group; avoid demanding many
tiny counteroffers. Buyer preference for filling inventory holes, price tier,
format and treatment informs module composition. Treat anchor leverage as a
negotiation hypothesis until paired offers support it. If the buyer accepts only
the core, preserve the remainder for another buyer or channel. All-or-nothing
conditions and quote revisions must be explicit. No stock changes at proposal.
