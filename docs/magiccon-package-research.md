# MagicCon buyer research and package strategy

Research checked October 4, 2026. Target: Atlanta, November 13–15, 2026. This extends the advance quote methodology. No buyers contacted, appointments booked, offers accepted or inventory allocated.

The strongest potential package is a buyer-specific purchase opportunity: desirable inventory the buyer actually needs, optional slower inventory with a measurable added offer, and a price agreed before travel. We should compete the desirable core, protect its outside value, and offer specialty cards to buyers who can monetize their exact treatments. Sending every card in one undifferentiated collection would conceal those distinctions.

## What the research supports

**Buyer demand is specific.** Published wanted quantities and exact cash bids are better evidence than a store's impressive retail assortment. A seller should distinguish a buyer's inventory hole from general card popularity. Historical dealer reporting describes hotlists as a way to identify the stock a booth most wants and compare its bids. This is a durable principle, not a current Atlanta hotlist. [TCGplayer vendor report](https://www.tcgplayer.com/content/article/A-Weekend-Behind-the-Vendor-Booth/8fb9604b-5410-4604-98a4-cabb4551947c/).

**Preparation can have financial value.** A current store policy explicitly pays more for a structured list and buylist matches, and allows higher negotiated rates for selected high-value cards. This is evidence that reduced processing work and buyer appetite can affect terms; its percentages are not Atlanta benchmarks. Its posted staple list also contains cards whose legality has changed, illustrating why a published list still requires validation. [Hex and Company acquisition policy](https://www.hexnyc.com/hextcg/).

**Cheap cards are not automatically worthless, but extracting their value can consume substantial effort.** An experienced seller's Atlanta report describes meaningful proceeds from organized low-price cards, alongside long processing and multiple vendor interactions. We should borrow the preparation discipline and move price discovery into advance quotes. We should not reproduce the on-site browsing process. [Quiet Speculation Atlanta field report](https://www.quietspeculation.com/2016/10/insider-gp-atlanta-vendor-report/).

**A desirable core can help a conversation, but a bundle premium is unproven.** The research does not establish that including good cards reliably raises the total paid for weaker cards. Buyer acceptance, cash improvement and convenience are three separate benefits. Our proposed paired-bid experiment below measures the second without assuming the first proves it.

**Use achievable alternatives.** Experienced buylisting guidance emphasizes comparing proceeds and work with individual selling. Dealers can have resale prices and operating efficiencies a private seller cannot replicate. Therefore neither TCG Mid nor a dealer's retail ask is the owner's cash alternative. Older fee examples are not reused as current costs. [Adam Yurchick on buylisting](https://www.quietspeculation.com/2014/12/insider-introduction-to-buylisting-how-and-why-to-buylist/).

**Prior relationships are useful evidence of process.** Experienced dealer advice emphasizes preparation, advance hotlists and relationships. For this owner, the previously successful fixed bundle is stronger process evidence than generic advice to visit booths and haggle. It does not establish a future quote or attendance. [Jeremy Aaranson on selling preparation](https://www.quietspeculation.com/2019/10/insider-how-youre-leaving-money-on-the-table/).

## Proposed package composition

| Role | Include when | Protect against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desirable core | Buyer wants exact variants and quantities; credible resale activity or current bid competition supports demand. | Sacrificing a superior existing cash bid just to make a bundle look attractive. |
| Specialist module | Exact-treatment completed evidence and buyer fit justify a collector price discussion. | Using ordinary-card popularity as proof of premium-printing liquidity. |
| Slower add-ons | Buyer offers worthwhile incremental cash and processing remains manageable. | Accepting a token amount for an open-ended box or hiding a core discount. |
| Retained cards | Supported hold case, meaningful patient self-sale advantage, or unresolved valuation with substantial dollars at stake. | Treating travel preparation as a reason to sell everything. |

These are package roles, not replacements for the owner's disposition decisions. A card can be a good buylist sale and a poor bargaining concession. A premium card can be a specialty inquiry without yet being an attractive anchor. Thin activity weakens price confidence; it does not prove a card has no buyers.

For every candidate, AI should explain the buyer's likely resale use, evidence supporting inclusion, stronger alternative, strongest counterargument and missing fact that could change the recommendation. Arithmetic should remain separate and visible. Do not turn a weighted desirability score into unexplained authority.

## Measure bundle leverage

For the same buyer, date, exact core and payment terms, request a core bid and one expanded bid. Avoid dozens of tiny permutations.

`incremental add-on cash = expanded package bid − core bid`

`add-on advantage = incremental add-on cash − credible add-on alternative net − incremental preparation cost`

Also compare the expanded total with the alternatives for the entire set. Illustrative numbers: core bid $1,000, expanded bid $1,180 and add-on fallback $120 gives a $60 add-on improvement before extra work. If the core itself can obtain $1,100 elsewhere, the expanded package's $1,180 loses $40 against $1,100 + $120. The attractive add-on increment did not save the whole deal. These numbers are hypothetical, not inventory valuations.

A buyer may offer only an all-or-nothing total. Evaluate it as a total; do not fabricate per-card allocations or claim proven subsidy. Removing an anchor requires a revised quote. Keep private fallback values and acceptance ranges out of the buyer packet.

## Fresh signals worth collecting

1. **Exact cash bids and remaining capacity:** scope, capture date, expiry, condition, currency and event applicability. Shared dealer capacity consumed once across identical variants.
2. **Buyer inventory holes:** an explicit wanted product beats general format rank. Repeated increases in bid or wanted quantity can justify negotiation attention, without implying a price forecast.
3. **Comparable completed USD sales:** same finish, language, treatment and grade; record shipping inclusion and transaction scope. Examine repeated bands and recency rather than one highest sale.
4. **Competitive supply:** ask distribution and quantities. Retail stock is useful context but not identical to buyer demand or a verified sell-through rate.
5. **Functional demand and confirmed catalysts:** tournament use, Commander adoption, actual published reprints or new synergies. Keep card-level demand distinct from exact-printing premium and shared upstream sources distinct from independent evidence.
6. **Buyer response evidence:** accepted/rejected variants, bid changes, accepted quantities and reasons. This becomes our most relevant buyer-fit telemetry over successive proposals.

## Practical proposal sequence

Build one comparable core from canonical stock, one specialist module and one manageable add-on module. Establish private fallback ranges and identify bids that need refresh. Qualify a small number of buyers for the required process before preparing bespoke full packets. Then compare actual written USD totals, exact accepted scope, exceptions and payment terms. Choose the allocation with worthwhile proceeds and few handoffs, preserving hold and patient self-sale choices.

The buyer sees a concise exact manifest, images where useful, offered quantities and delivery window. The owner sees AI inclusion reasoning, cash alternatives, credible self-sale net, counterarguments, incremental module value and total package economics. No general disclaimer wall belongs in the main interface.

Research completion establishes a defensible proposal design and buyer discovery map. A final travel package still requires fresh exact bids, qualified counterparties and agreed advance terms. The full 10-buyer source ledger and 11 strategy findings are retained alongside this document; historical articles and anecdotal reports are contextual, not current pricing authority.
